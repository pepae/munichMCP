# Stadtbezirk 01: Altstadt-Lehel

Altstadt-Lehel is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 1, home to 20,606 registered main residents on 3.1 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 1 (BA 1), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
