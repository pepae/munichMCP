# Stadtbezirk 07: Sendling-Westpark

Sendling-Westpark is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 7, home to 62,640 registered main residents on 7.8 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 7 (BA 7), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
