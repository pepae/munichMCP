# Stadtbezirk 09: Neuhausen-Nymphenburg

Neuhausen-Nymphenburg is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 9, home to 102,527 registered main residents on 12.9 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 9 (BA 9), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
