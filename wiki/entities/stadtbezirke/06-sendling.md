# Stadtbezirk 06: Sendling

Sendling is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 6, home to 41,334 registered main residents on 3.9 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 6 (BA 6), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
