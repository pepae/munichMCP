# Stadtbezirk 11: Milbertshofen-Am Hart

Milbertshofen-Am Hart is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 11, home to 77,500 registered main residents on 13.4 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 11 (BA 11), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
