# Stadtbezirk 15: Trudering-Riem

Trudering-Riem is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 15, home to 77,154 registered main residents on 22.5 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 15 (BA 15), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
