# Stadtbezirk 23: Allach-Untermenzing

Allach-Untermenzing is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 23, home to 36,238 registered main residents on 15.5 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 23 (BA 23), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
