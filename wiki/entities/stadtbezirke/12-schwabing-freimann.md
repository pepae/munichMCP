# Stadtbezirk 12: Schwabing-Freimann

Schwabing-Freimann is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 12, home to 76,809 registered main residents on 25.7 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 12 (BA 12), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
