# Stadtbezirk 14: Berg am Laim

Berg am Laim is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 14, home to 47,185 registered main residents on 6.3 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 14 (BA 14), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
