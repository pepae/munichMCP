# Stadtbezirk 21: Pasing-Obermenzing

Pasing-Obermenzing is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 21, home to 81,783 registered main residents on 16.5 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 21 (BA 21), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
