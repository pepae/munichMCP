# Stadtbezirk 25: Laim

Laim is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 25, home to 57,515 registered main residents on 5.3 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 25 (BA 25), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
