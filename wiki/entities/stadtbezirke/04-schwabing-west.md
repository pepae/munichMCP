# Stadtbezirk 04: Schwabing-West

Schwabing-West is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 4, home to 69,484 registered main residents on 4.4 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 4 (BA 4), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
