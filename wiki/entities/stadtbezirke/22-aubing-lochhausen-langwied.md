# Stadtbezirk 22: Aubing-Lochhausen-Langwied

Aubing-Lochhausen-Langwied is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 22, home to 63,207 registered main residents on 34.1 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 22 (BA 22), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
