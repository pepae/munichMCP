# Stadtbezirk 18: Untergiesing-Harlaching

Untergiesing-Harlaching is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 18, home to 54,227 registered main residents on 8.1 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 18 (BA 18), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
