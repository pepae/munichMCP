# Stadtbezirk 13: Bogenhausen

Bogenhausen is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 13, home to 95,540 registered main residents on 23.7 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 13 (BA 13), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
