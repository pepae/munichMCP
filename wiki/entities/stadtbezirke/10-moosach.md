# Stadtbezirk 10: Moosach

Moosach is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 10, home to 56,150 registered main residents on 11.1 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 10 (BA 10), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
