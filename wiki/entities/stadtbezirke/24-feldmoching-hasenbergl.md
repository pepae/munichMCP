# Stadtbezirk 24: Feldmoching-Hasenbergl

Feldmoching-Hasenbergl is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 24, home to 64,586 registered main residents on 28.9 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 24 (BA 24), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
