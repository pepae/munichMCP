# Stadtbezirk 03: Maxvorstadt

Maxvorstadt is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 3, home to 52,501 registered main residents on 4.3 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 3 (BA 3), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
