# Stadtbezirk 02: Ludwigsvorstadt-Isarvorstadt

Ludwigsvorstadt-Isarvorstadt is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 2, home to 50,048 registered main residents on 4.4 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 2 (BA 2), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
