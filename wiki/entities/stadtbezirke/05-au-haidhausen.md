# Stadtbezirk 05: Au-Haidhausen

Au-Haidhausen is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 5, home to 64,122 registered main residents on 4.2 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 5 (BA 5), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
