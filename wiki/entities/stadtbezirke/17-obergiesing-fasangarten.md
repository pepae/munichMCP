# Stadtbezirk 17: Obergiesing-Fasangarten

Obergiesing-Fasangarten is Munich's city district (Stadtbezirk) no. 17, home to 54,500 registered main residents on 5.7 km² (Statistisches Amt München, 2025). Its local council is the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse|Bezirksausschuss]] 17 (BA 17), which files motions to the city (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
