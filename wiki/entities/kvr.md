# Kreisverwaltungsreferat (KVR)

The KVR is the city department responsible for many everyday administrative services, including:

- registration of residence (**Bürgerbüro**): [[topics/anmeldung]];
- **resident parking permits**: [[topics/bewohnerparken]];
- the district inspection (*Bezirksinspektion*), present at [[topics/buergerversammlungen]].

Bürgerbüro visits need an **appointment**. Many services are also online: the city publishes the full list of its online services as open data (465 entries in the October 2025 list), which munichMCP searches alongside the service directory.

munichMCP does **not** book or scrape appointments. Automated appointment booking hurts residents by hoarding slots.
