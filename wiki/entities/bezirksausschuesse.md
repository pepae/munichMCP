# District committees (*Bezirksausschüsse*, BA)

Each of Munich's 25 [[entities/stadtbezirke|city districts]] has an elected district committee with the same number (BA 1 Altstadt-Lehel to BA 25 Laim). The BA is the closest political body to residents: it is consulted on local projects, decides some local matters and budgets, and passes **BA motions** (*BA-Anträge*) to the city administration and the [[entities/stadtrat]].

- BA motions are numbered like the council's, e.g. "BA-Antrag Nr. 26-32 / B 00368" for the 2026-2032 term. On 24 September 2026 alone, more than 20 new BA motions were published.
- The BA chair co-organises the district's [[topics/buergerversammlungen|residents' assembly]] and reports there.
- BA meetings are public; dates are published as open data (dataset "Bezirksausschuss Sitzungen").
- Several BAs share an office (*Geschäftsstelle*), e.g. Geschäftsstelle Ost for BAs 6, 7, 8, 18 and 19 at Meindlstraße 14.

Follow them with `council_search` (body `bezirksausschuss`) or in the RIS.

## What district committees are asking about (September 2026)

Themes that came up in several districts at once (motions are requests to the administration, not decisions):

- **The city's consolidation process** (*AG Zukunftssicherung*): BAs 8 Schwanthalerhöhe, 13 Bogenhausen, 16 Ramersdorf-Perlach and 18 Untergiesing-Harlaching ask what it will mean for social services in their districts, and BA 5 Au-Haidhausen asks to be involved in the process. Background: [[topics/stadthaushalt]].
- **The yellow recycling bin** (*Gelbe Tonne*): BA 16 Ramersdorf-Perlach asks the waste management company AWM to present the planned introduction, and BA 14 Berg am Laim asks for simple information for residents on the bins themselves.
- **Heat protection**: BA 8 for the square on the Theresienhöhe railway cover, BA 13 for the Rosenkavalierplatz and for the renovation of the former Wilhelm-Hausenstein-Gymnasium.
- **Large development areas** (*städtebauliche Entwicklungsmaßnahmen*, SEM): BA 13 Bogenhausen asks for the feasibility studies on the SEM Nordost to be presented publicly; BA 24 Feldmoching-Hasenbergl asks for planning on the SEM München Nord to be stopped.
- **Shared e-scooters and bikes** blocking footpaths: see [[topics/e-scooter-und-sharing]].
