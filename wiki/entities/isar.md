# Isar

The Isar crosses the city for about 14 km from south-west to north-east. It is Munich's main recreation space, with official bathing and boating zones (see [[topics/baden-in-der-isar]]).

## Munich gauge (Pegel München, 16005701)

Operated by the Bavarian Environment Agency (LfU). It reports water level, discharge and water temperature every 15 minutes. The flood warning levels (*Meldestufen*) are:

| Meldestufe | Water level |
|---|---|
| 1 | 240 cm |
| 2 | 300 cm |
| 3 | 380 cm |
| 4 | 520 cm |

On 25 September 2026 at 16:00 the gauge read 91 cm, 21.6 m³/s and 15.1 °C: far below any warning level.

`isar_conditions` shows the current values, the official flood class and the bathing rules for a spot.
