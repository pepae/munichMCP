# Munich City Council (*Stadtrat*)

The Stadtrat is Munich's elected council. Under Bavarian municipal law a city of more than one million inhabitants has **80 councillors**; they are joined by the directly elected **Oberbürgermeister**, currently **Dominik Krause** (GRÜNE), who won the runoff on 22 March 2026 with 56.3 %. Election results: [[topics/wahlen-2026]]. The current term runs **2026 to 2032**: motions filed in this term carry numbers like "26-32 / A 00210".

## How it works

- The **full assembly** (*Vollversammlung*) takes the major decisions. It meets in the Großer Sitzungssaal of the Rathaus and is **livestreamed** at muenchen.de/stadtrat-live, with sign-language interpretation. Example: 30 September 2026, 9:00.
- **Committees** prepare and decide within their remit (e.g. Finanzausschuss, Mobilitätsausschuss, Sozialausschuss). The Mobilitätsausschuss adopted the cycling network level IR III on 23 September 2026 ([[topics/radverkehrsnetz]]).
- Councillors and groups file **motions** (*Anträge*, number with "A") and **inquiries** (*Anfragen*, "F"). The departments (*Referate*) answer; answers are also published in the Rathaus Umschau ("Antwort auf Stadtratsanfrage").

## Recent decisions (full assembly, 30 September 2026)

- Supplementary budget 2026 adopted: deficit of €638 m in the results budget, investment payments and new loans reduced. See [[topics/stadthaushalt]].
- Continuation of the Tram Münchner Norden and the Tram-Westtangente plan with the existing Aidenbachstraße P+R garage. See [[entities/mvg-mvv]].
- Third LED programme: 30,000 more street lights, €26.6 m.
- Next planning steps for bridge renewals, including a design competition for a new Donnersbergerbrücke.
- Florian Kraus re-elected head of the education and sports department (62 of 76 valid votes), second term from 1 February 2027.
