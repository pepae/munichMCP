# Swimming and boating in the Isar

The [[entities/isar]] flows about 14 km through the city from south-west to north-east. **Bathing is allowed only in designated zones and always at your own risk.** The city strongly advises against entering the water at high water (strong current, debris). Bathing is prohibited near weirs, power-station intakes, spillways, locks, whirlpools and storm-water outlets.

## Zones

The city's map marks where bathing is **allowed** (*erlaubt*) or **prohibited** (*verboten*): several sections of the Isar allow it, while side waters such as the Eisbach, Schwabinger Bach, Kleine Isar, Kleinhesseloher See and most canals prohibit it. Boating zones follow the *Bade- und Bootverordnung* of 21 December 1976; since a general order of 02.04.2020 boating through the Floßgasse at the Marienklausensteg is banned.

## Danger spots

The city lists specific danger points, for example the weirs at the Flauchersteg, the Wittelsbacher Brücke (no swimming 20 m upstream and 40 m downstream) and the Heizkraftwerk weir, plus stretches where swimming is only allowed on the eastern bank. `isar_conditions` shows those near you.

## Is it safe today?

`isar_conditions` combines:

- **Water level, discharge and water temperature** at gauge München (16005701) from the Bavarian flood service (HND/GKD). On 25.09.2026, 16:00: 91 cm, 21.6 m³/s, 15.1 °C.
- **Flood warning levels** at this gauge: Meldestufe 1 at 240 cm, 2 at 300 cm, 3 at 380 cm, 4 at 520 cm.
- The official **flood class** from the cross-state flood portal (Datenquelle: www.hochwasserzentralen.de).
- Weather and DWD warnings.
