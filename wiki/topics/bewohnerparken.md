# Resident parking permits (*Bewohnerparkausweis*)

Large parts of inner Munich are **parking licence areas** (*Parklizenzgebiete*, part of the city's *Parkraummanagement*). There, residents without a private parking space can get a permit to park in their area. `address_report` tells you whether an address lies in such an area and which one; the district pages list them (e.g. [[entities/stadtbezirke/05-au-haidhausen]]).

## Who can apply

- You are registered with your residence in the licence area (see [[topics/anmeldung]]).
- You have a vehicle registered to you, or one you demonstrably use permanently (for company or carsharing cars, with proof).
- You have **no private parking space**.
- Trailers and vehicles built mainly for commercial use cannot be entered; foreign plates normally not either.

One permit per resident, with up to three vehicles entered.

## How to apply and what it costs

- Fastest: the [online application](https://service.muenchen.de/intelliform/forms/01/02/02/bewohnerparkausweis/index), uploading the vehicle registration (part I) and, if needed, proof of use.
- Online applications submitted before 11:00 can be processed the same day; a printed confirmation in the car is tolerated for up to a week.
- Fees per the city's service article: **30 euros for 12 months, 60 euros for 24 months**. Renewal: you get a reminder about six weeks before expiry and simply pay.
- Hardship exceptions exist, e.g. regular care of a relative in the area, night shifts when public transport does not run, frequent hospital treatment.

Responsible department: [[entities/kvr]]. Legal basis: § 45 (1b) no. 2a StVO and the council's decisions on parking management (see [[entities/stadtrat]]).
