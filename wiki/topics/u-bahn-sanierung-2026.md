# U-Bahn renewal works, autumn 2026 (*U-Bahn-Sanierung*)

As part of the programme to renew Munich's U-Bahn (*Grunderneuerung*), Stadtwerke München (SWM) work on several sections from **Monday, 5 October 2026**. Source: MVG press release of 1 October 2026, published in the Rathaus Umschau.

## U3 and U6: Poccistraße and Goetheplatz (until 27 November)

Second of three closure phases for the renovation of the Poccistraße and Goetheplatz stations. Only one track is closed this time:

- **U3** interrupted between **Sendlinger Tor and Implerstraße**; **U6** interrupted between **Goetheplatz and Implerstraße**.
- A **shuttle train** (*Pendelzug*) runs between Implerstraße and Goetheplatz; **replacement buses** run between Implerstraße and Sendlinger Tor or Hauptbahnhof.
- MVG advice: U3 passengers use the U3 replacement bus; U6 passengers use the shuttle train.
- Regular service from **28 November to 11 April**. The third and last phase runs from **12 April to 1 August 2027**, with all tracks closed again.

## U4 and U5: Max-Weber-Platz (until 27 November)

Two escalators are replaced, so fire-safety rules allow only limited passenger exchange:

- **U4** split into two sections: Arabellapark to Max-Weber-Platz, and Odeonsplatz to Westendstraße via Hauptbahnhof. **Lehel** is not served.
- **U5** passes Max-Weber-Platz towards Hauptbahnhof and Laimer Platz without stopping. From Ostbahnhof, tram 21 reaches Max-Weber-Platz.
- Alternatives: trams 16 and 17 from Arabellapark to Karlsplatz (Stachus) and Hauptbahnhof (tram 16 runs to St. Emmeram on weekdays from about 6:00 to 21:00); U4 to Prinzregentenplatz and bus 100 to Odeonsplatz; trams 19 and 21 from Max-Weber-Platz into the city centre.

## U4, U5 and U8: Neuperlach (until 8 November)

Points and about 35 other measures at Neuperlach Süd and Therese-Giehse-Allee:

- **U5** runs only between Laimer Platz and Neuperlach Zentrum; **replacement buses** between Neuperlach Zentrum and Neuperlach Süd.
- **U8** is diverted onto the U2 route from Innsbrucker Ring via Trudering to Messestadt Ost; Michaelibad, Quiddestraße and Neuperlach Zentrum are served by the U5.
- MVG recommends the **S5** (Neuperlach Süd, Giesing, Ostbahnhof, Hauptbahnhof, every 20 minutes) to avoid the replacement buses.
