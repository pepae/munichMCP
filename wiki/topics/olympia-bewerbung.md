# Munich's Olympic bid (*Olympiabewerbung*)

Munich is Germany's candidate to host the **Olympic and Paralympic Summer Games in 2036, 2040 or 2044**. At an extraordinary general meeting of the German Olympic Sports Confederation (DOSB) in Baden-Baden on **26 September 2026**, Munich won the national selection against Köln-Rhein-Ruhr by **91 to 50 votes**. The bid from Bavaria is now reported to the International Olympic Committee (IOC). (Rathaus Umschau, 28 September 2026)

## What the city says about its concept

- The concept builds on **existing venues** and links the Games with long-term development goals for the city, according to sports department head Florian Kraus.
- Mayor (*Oberbürgermeister*) Dominik Krause said the concept had convinced Munich's residents "last year" and now the German sports movement, and that the city and the Free State of Bavaria would continue to work closely together.
- The bid is managed by the education and sports department (*Referat für Bildung und Sport*, RBS), whose head was re-elected by the city council on 30 September 2026.

## Next steps

1. The national concept is turned into an **international bid** together with the DOSB, the Free State of Bavaria and the federal government.
2. The goal is to enter the IOC's **Strategic Dialogue**, expected in **spring 2027**. The IOC redesigned its procedure for international bids in June 2026, but has not yet defined it in detail.
3. The city expects to give details on the working structures in the **second half of October 2026**.
