# Munich's cycling network

Munich is building a hierarchical cycling network following the *Empfehlungen für Radverkehrsanlagen* (ERA 2010), from category **IR II** down to **IR V**. Categories IR II to IR IV together form the "main connections" (*Hauptverbindungen*) and correspond to the goal of the **Radentscheid** citizens' initiative.

## September 2026: cross-district routes adopted

On **23 September 2026** the council's mobility committee adopted the **IR III** level: routes that connect central points with the city centre and with each other, for example the Altstadt with Harras or the Arabellapark with Münchner Freiheit. For IR III, one-way cycle paths have a standard width of **2.5 m**. The network is used to safeguard routes in new plans, to prioritise measures and to find gaps.

- The approach for the IR II routes had already been decided.
- The remaining connections are to go to the council in **2027**.
- The routes were drafted under the lead of the Mobilitätsreferat with city departments, neighbouring districts (*Landkreise*), the [[entities/bezirksausschuesse]] and representatives of the Radentscheid.

Quotes in the press release come from Bürgermeisterin Mona Fuchs (safety for children, seniors and other vulnerable road users; closing gaps quickly and cheaply) and Mobilitätsreferent Georg Dunkel.

## Related open data

The Mobilitätsreferat publishes the Radentscheid measures, fast cycle routes (*Radschnellverbindungen*), bicycle streets, the Altstadt-Radlring, bike counters and the "green wave" for cyclists as open geodata. Live counts: `find_nearby` category `bike_counter`.
