# Milieuschutz areas (*Erhaltungssatzungen*)

In a Milieuschutz area the city aims to preserve the **composition of the resident population** (§ 172 (1) sentence 1 no. 2 BauGB). Munich's statutes have no end date, but the city reviews every five years whether each area still qualifies.

## What needs approval

In these areas the following need a permit from the city, for owner-occupied, rented and empty flats alike:

- **Structural changes**, e.g. modernising bathrooms, changing floor plans, installing lifts.
- **Energy measures** under the Buildings Energy Act (GEG), e.g. new windows or doors, insulation, new heating.
- **Changes of use**, e.g. turning a flat into an office or practice.
- **Demolition** or partial demolition of housing.
- **Conversion into condominiums** (*Wohnungseigentum*) needs a separate approval.

Pure repairs, an attic conversion that does not touch existing housing, and changes to non-residential rooms generally do not. The statute itself does **not cap rents**; the exception is buildings bound by an agreement to avert the city's pre-emption right.

## How to check an address

`address_report` returns whether an address lies in an Erhaltungssatzung area, its name, since when it applies, and links to the official map, statute and justification PDFs. Example: Wörthstraße 10 lies in the area "Haidhausen" (since 11.03.2021).

The data comes from the Referat für Stadtplanung und Bauordnung. It is not a substitute for the official site plan.
