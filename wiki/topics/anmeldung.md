# Registering your address (*Wohnsitz anmelden*)

Anyone who moves to Munich, or moves within Munich, must register the new address. The law (*Bundesmeldegesetz*) gives **14 days** after moving in. The responsible department is the [[entities/kvr|Kreisverwaltungsreferat (KVR)]].

## Online or in person

- **Online** at [wohnsitzanmeldung.gov.de](https://wohnsitzanmeldung.gov.de/), but only for the **main residence** (*Hauptwohnsitz*).
- **In person** at a Bürgerbüro, **with an appointment** (booked on the city's appointment page). A second residence (*Nebenwohnsitz*) always needs a visit. You can send someone with a written power of attorney.
- Families: one person subject to registration may register the whole household.

## What to bring

- A valid ID document for every person being registered.
- The landlord's confirmation (*Wohnungsgeberbestätigung*): address, name and address of the landlord (and of the owner if different), move-in date and the names of everyone moving in. If you do not have it yet, you can submit a declaration that it is missing.
- If Munich becomes your main residence: ID card, and when moving to Munich also your passport, so the address can be updated.
- Car owners moving within Munich: vehicle registration certificate part I and proof of a valid inspection, to update the vehicle papers.

## Rules worth knowing

- You can only register after you have actually moved in (no future dates).
- Deregistration at the old address in Germany happens automatically.
- If you have several flats in Germany, you must decide which is the main residence.

## Fees and what happens next

- Registration is **free**; updating the address in vehicle papers costs 12 euros.
- After a first registration in Germany, the tax ID (*Steuer-Identifikationsnummer*) arrives by post from the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern after roughly 2 to 4 weeks.
