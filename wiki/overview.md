# munichMCP wiki

This wiki is munichMCP's compiled memory about Munich: explanations, how-tos and current developments, written by maintainer agents from primary sources and linked together. Where munichMCP's other tools give live data (departures, rules at an address, statistics), the wiki gives the **context**: what a Milieuschutz area means, how the Mietspiegel works, what the council decided this week.

**How it stays current.** A collector captures every new council and district-committee motion, every Rathaus Umschau press release and every new open dataset into an inbox every day. A maintainer agent regularly compiles that inbox into pages, following the [[schema]], and checks the link graph. Every change is logged in [[log]] and versioned. The full catalogue is in [[index]].

## This week in Munich (as of 2 October 2026)

- **Olympic bid:** Munich is Germany's candidate for the 2036, 2040 or 2044 Summer Olympics and Paralympics. The German Olympic Sports Confederation (DOSB) chose Munich over Köln-Rhein-Ruhr by 91 to 50 votes on 26 September. See [[topics/olympia-bewerbung]].
- **City finances:** on 30 September the council adopted the 2026 supplementary budget (*Nachtragshaushalt*): ordinary expenses €10.67 bn, income €10.03 bn, a deficit of €638 m covered from reserves; investment payments cut by €304 m and new loans by €460 m. See [[topics/stadthaushalt]].
- **U-Bahn works from 5 October:** U3 and U6 interrupted around Implerstraße (shuttle train and replacement buses), U4 split at Max-Weber-Platz, U5 not stopping there towards the city centre, both until 27 November; replacement buses between Neuperlach Zentrum and Neuperlach Süd and U8 diverted until 8 November. See [[topics/u-bahn-sanierung-2026]].
- **Tunnels:** night closures (22:00 to 5:30) of the Mittlerer Ring Ost tunnels (Effner, Richard-Strauss, Leuchtenbergring, Innsbrucker Ring) from 5 to 10 October, and of the Altstadtringtunnel on the nights of 5 to 8 October.
- **Trams:** the council approved continuing the Tram Münchner Norden (Schwabing Nord to Kieferngarten, opening planned for the end of 2031) and keeping the P+R garage at Aidenbachstraße for the Tram-Westtangente (opening still planned for the end of 2028). Info evening on the northern tram: 6 October, 18:30, Gymnasium Neufreimann, Friederike-Nadig-Allee 48. See [[entities/mvg-mvv]].
- **Autonomous bus:** an automated MAN electric bus runs on line 178 (Petuelring to Freimanner Hölzl) with safety drivers on board; a pilot with selected test passengers is planned from November (research project MINGA).
- **Council decisions:** 30,000 more street lights switch to LED (€26.6 m), because conventional high-pressure lamps may no longer be sold in the EU from 2027; next planning steps for bridge renewals (Donnersbergerbrücke, Hackerbrücke, Thalkirchner Brücke, the suspension bridge over Schenkendorfstraße); Florian Kraus re-elected head of the education and sports department (62 of 76 votes). See [[entities/stadtrat]].
- **Residents' assemblies:** Sendling-Westpark on 8 October and Moosach on 12 October (both 19:00, with a Q&A hour from 18:00), then Obergiesing-Fasangarten (20 October), Hadern (22 October) and Bogenhausen (27 October). District committees meeting in public next week include Sendling (5 October), Ludwigsvorstadt-Isarvorstadt and Pasing-Obermenzing (6 October) and Laim (8 October). See [[topics/buergerversammlungen]].
- **Council committees next week:** 6 October (social, youth welfare, education, culture and building committees), 7 October (mobility and planning, sport, education), 8 October (audit, municipal and culture committees).
- **Jobs:** unemployment in the Munich employment agency district was 5.3 % in September (61,124 people), 0.1 points above a year earlier; employers reported 10,452 vacancies, 26.7 % more than a year ago. The press release is titled "August" but reports September figures.
- **Families and leisure:** the holiday pass (*Ferienpass*) 2026/27 is sold digitally from 5 October, and low-income families get a voucher code for a free pass at the usual issuing offices. A city survey of single parents runs online until the end of November. The free indoor sports programme "MUCkis für alle" started on 1 October in more than 40 halls. The Westbad indoor pool and sauna reopen on 14 October.
